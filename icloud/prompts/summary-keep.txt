The user already sees the sender and subject. Don't repeat them, and don't describe the email ("The email is from…", "It mentions…"): give what the body says.
In at most TWO plain sentences, give the specific content: what happened or changed, concrete facts, numbers and names from the body, and what, if anything, the user needs to do and by when.
If the body adds nothing beyond the subject, reply with exactly: No details beyond the subject.
Example. Subject: "Session Summary - November 25". Bad: "The email is from the tutoring center and summarizes the student's session on November 25." Good: "Completed 3 pages and 1 skill check; the tutor suggests more practice with fractions before next week."
